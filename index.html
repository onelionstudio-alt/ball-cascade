Atelier 452

Build. Launch. Learn.

Move modules in the flow preview. Drag the ↔ arrow left or right to aim. Start a challenge when your route is ready.

Each attempt has a finite ball budget. Lit pockets are the targets. Gray pockets keep balls safe, but may not meet your objective. Red teeth destroy balls. Icons also mark every hazard.

A root ball counts once per target pocket, even after splitting. Target efficiency counts how many original balls reached any target. First clears and new stars earn coins; identical repeats do not.

Failed attempts cost no coins or upgrades. Reloading restarts an unfinished attempt; completed levels and layouts stay saved.
